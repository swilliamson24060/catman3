# Seasonal Resonance Asset Batch E — Technical Delivery Brief

Milestone 7 locks the First Bloom solver, plinth/component sockets, five-tier feedback language, and activation transformation. Production art must preserve the tested geometry and communicate progress without exposing hidden component roles as a checklist.

## Required set

- Three standing-stone plinths with ground origin, separate collision proxy, `ComponentSlot`, `VFXSocket`, and interaction anchor.
- Activated and inactive materials for each plinth plus rain lens, carved copper gear, and heirloom seed components.
- Tier-specific VFX for Dormant, Stirring, Echoing, Aligned, and Activated; color-independent motion/shape distinctions are required for the later accessibility pass.
- Three-note spatial audio motif with restrained variations for tiers 1–4.
- First Bloom flower palette/models, old-tree pulse/rustle treatment, water/light line treatment, and a short activation sequence.
- Resident listen/notice/celebrate reaction clips. The full celebration event remains a separate Milestone 9 delivery.

## Locked contracts

- Godot scale is 1 unit = 1 meter. Plinth origin is ground center; component slot is 0.9 m high and VFX socket is 1.1 m high.
- The introductory triangle accepts 3.5–5.5 m sides with 0.65 m tolerance and rotation-independent world-space evaluation. Art and collision must not change socket positions.
- Component silhouettes must remain distinct at gameplay zoom. Gameplay identity and roles come from data, never mesh or material names.
- Feedback lines use separate replaceable scene nodes; particles and tones must not determine solver state.
- Provide GLB sources, material variants, texture/VFX budgets, audio source/license data, and an isolated import-validation scene.
- All placeholders retain `development_placeholder` metadata until scale, pivot, sockets, materials, animation names, and performance are validated.

Do not include irrigation-machine states or dye/craft assets in Batch E; those belong to Milestone 8.
